# Sets 3 & 4 (Golden Hour Beach, Coast Road) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ship Sets 3 and 4. Each is a world, a visual, an original built-in instrument and an ambience tied by one mood, both inspired by songs from Lana Del Rey's *Honeymoon*.

**Architecture:**
- The run adds to what Sets 1 & 2 built.
- `InStrumDSP` gains two engines (Honey Tape and Haze Bells) behind the existing C API, plus seven ambience sources with wave and speed controls.
- `InStrumCore` gains:
  - a per-world sky hold
  - a deterministic ocean swell
  - two visual simulations (Tide Lines and Coast Lights)
  - two world models
  - the moods, credits, patches and sets
- `InStrumApp` gains a shared Ocean shading layer, two world renderers and two living cards.
- The built-in Audio Unit, panel, picker, mapping and sets plumbing are already generic: each new engine is an enum case, a component subtype and a parameter table.

**Tech Stack:**
- Swift 5.10 tools (Swift 6.1 compiler, Swift 5 mode), SwiftPM.
- C++17 (`InStrumDSP`).
- AVFoundation / AudioToolbox.
- Metal (runtime-compiled MSL, `RenderStack`).
- SwiftUI.
- XCTest.
- GitHub Actions macos-15.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-sets-3-4-design.md` (read it alongside this plan).

## Global Constraints

**Platform and dependencies**
- macOS 13.0 deployment. The release binary is x86_64 (Intel). CI tests run on macos-15 (Apple silicon). All code builds for both.
- No third-party dependencies. No recordings or samples of any song: every sound is synthesised.

**Sound Core rules (unchanged)**
- No allocation, locks or logging on the audio thread.
- Denormals flushed (`DenormalGuard`).
- Sample-accurate MIDI.
- Every dial smoothed (`Smoothed`, 20 ms).
- Voices stolen quietest-first with a 20 ms fade.

**Instruments**
- **Tuning:** A = 440 Hz, accurate within **2 cents**. Fine tune **±50 cents** and Transpose **±12**, exact within **1 cent**.
- **Polyphony:** Honey Tape **16**, Haze Bells **24**.
- **CPU (CI):**
  - Honey Tape 12-note sustained chord **< 5%** of real time.
  - Haze Bells 24-note pedalled run **< 4%**.
  - Beach or road ambience at full **< 2%**.

**Ambience**
- Ducking −4 dB while playing, about 2 s recovery.
- Cross-fades ≥ 2 s.
- Panic never silences ambience.
- **Gulls default to off.**

**Visuals**
- **Smooth rules:**
  - ≤ 1 MP
  - no per-pixel ray marching
  - noise ≤ 3 octaves (2 on Smooth for the ocean)
  - simulations analytic or at quarter resolution
- Glints, foam glow, lamp light and glitter bloom go through `FlashLimiter` (sparkle halved when Gentle).

**Credits**
- Text and a Spotify link only. No artwork, logos, photos, lyrics or music-video imagery.
- Card art is original, from the song's feeling, with tempo, key and track number as easter eggs.

**Names**
- **Sets:**
  - `golden-hour-beach` / **Golden Hour Beach** / mood *Warm, hazy, nostalgic*
  - `coast-road` / **Coast Road** / mood *Hazy, restless, free*
- **Instruments:** **Honey Tape** (`honeyTape`, subtype `isHT`) and **Haze Bells** (`hazeBells`, subtype `isHB`).
- **Visuals:** **Tide Lines** and **Coast Lights**.
- **Credits:** `music-to-watch-boys-to` and `high-by-the-beach`.
- **Hold the light:** choices "Follow the sky", "Golden hour" (sun at **5°**) and "Blue hour" (sun at **−6°**).

## Review Focus

1. **Sample-rate change and odd host buffers on the new engines.** The tape delay and echo buffers must be sized for 96 kHz and stay in tune at 44.1 kHz. Pinned in Task 2 and Task 4 by extending `BuiltInAUTests.testTuningHoldsAt44_1And96k` and `testRendersAnyBufferSize` to every engine.
2. **Old Reel with the pedal down, and panic during a reel fade.** Notes must still run out, and nothing gets stuck. Pinned in Task 1 (`testReelRunsOutEvenWithThePedal`, `testPanicDuringReelFadeLeavesNoVoices`).
3. **Unusual window shapes:** ultra-wide 2.4:1 and square 1:1. Screen-space layers (palms, guardrail, lamps, keys) must still frame a sensible picture. Pinned in Task 9 and Task 11 (`testBeachFramesAnyAspect`, `testRoadFramesAnyAspect`).
4. **Hours-long sessions.** Swell phases and shader time must not jitter after 10 hours (the Float-precision minor from Sets 1 & 2). Pinned in Task 9 (`testOceanStaysSmoothAfterHours`).
5. **Leaving Coast Road for another world.** The road and engine sound must glide down, not stay at speed; a pending wave must not fire into the next world. Pinned in Task 7 (`testSpeedAndWaveSurviveTheThrottle`, `testSpeedFallsWhenTheWorldChanges`).

## Rulings recorded while planning

- **Order:** the ambience sources (Task 7) come **before** the worlds (spec §13 listed them after). That way each world task wires its own soundscape against real sources, and the exhaustive `AmbienceSource` switch in `AmbienceAU` compiles at every commit. *Cost if wrong:* none (the same work).
- **Cards:** each card lands with its instrument's app task (Task 2 and Task 4), not in Task 12. The built-in panel shows the card, and the card tests iterate `CreditLibrary.all`, so a credit without art would fail them.
- **Gulls "off by default":** a beach world parameter, `gulls`, defaulting to 0.
- **Wave events:** they pass through the existing 10 Hz ambience throttle like thunder. They are held, never dropped, and can arrive up to 100 ms after the picture's break. *Cost if wrong:* a just-audible lag, which would mean moving the swell into the DSP.
- **Surf distance:** a new ambience control set by the world (beach 0.15, road 0.9), separate from the user's closeness.
- **Echo time:** Haze Bells' echo time is a constant from the brief (`HazeBells::kEchoSeconds`, a dotted-eighth at the song's tempo), not a dial.
- **Shared identifiers:** Honey Tape reuses the `space` (2) and `width` (14) parameter addresses, and Haze Bells reuses `space` (2) and `drift` (21). The tables are per engine.

- **`TapeKit.hpp`** (namespace `isd`):
  - `struct TapeDelay { void prepare(double sr); void setDepth(float wowCents, float flutterCents); float process(float x); }`: a 6 ms base delay, modulated, with cubic interpolation.
    - A sinusoidal delay swing of ±D seconds at f Hz gives a pitch swing of ±2πfD. Size D from the cents: `D = (2^(c/1200) − 1) / (2πf)`.
    - Wow runs at 0.4–0.8 Hz (slowly wandering) and flutter at 6–10 Hz.
  - `struct TapeSaturator { void setAge(float a); float process(float x); }`: soft saturation, a high shelf/low-pass from about 16 kHz (age 0) down to about 6 kHz (age 1), a +2 dB head bump at about 100 Hz, and hiss up to −62 dBFS at age 1.
  - `struct BitCrusher` and `struct FilteredEcho` are added in Task 3.
- `class HoneyTape final : public Engine` with `kPolyphony = 16`, following `OrbitPad`'s voice, steal and housekeeping pattern.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** in `HoneyTapeTests.swift`. Use `SynthRender` and `DSPTestKit`, with `dry = [ISD_P_SPACE: 0]`. Use flute-only (`strings 0`, `wow 0`, `debugNoVibrato 1`) where pitch matters.
