# Sets 5 & 6 (Snowbound Cabin Window, Bedroom Window at Dawn) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ship Sets 5 and 6, each with one of everything:
- a world, a visual and an original instrument
- a new sky feature, a new weather and new ambience sounds

The new sky and weather reach every world with a sky.

**Architecture:**
- The run adds to what Sets 1–4 built.
- **`InStrumDSP`:**
  - Felt Glass's modal strings move into a shared header. Felt Glass's sound is locked by a fingerprint first.
  - Two engines are added: Cabin Felt (an upright felt piano with a top-voice horn) and Morning Box (a celesta or music box over a synth wall that builds).
  - Five ambience sources are added, plus an indoor "through the glass" filter, wet-road hiss and snow ticks.
- **`InStrumCore`:**
  - Two weathers and new sky values.
  - Pastel dawns and halo optics.
  - The frost field, the cloth simulation, two visual simulations and two world models.
- **`InStrumApp`:**
  - Halos, the Belt of Venus and blizzards in the shared sky and weather layers.
  - Wet ground on the beach and road.
  - A reusable Window Kit, two world renderers and two living cards.
- The built-in Audio Unit, panel, picker, mapping and sets plumbing are already generic.

## Global Constraints

**Platform and dependencies**
- macOS 13.0 deployment. The release binary is x86_64 (Intel). CI tests run on macos-15 (Apple silicon). All code builds for both.
- No third-party dependencies. No recordings or samples of any song: every sound is synthesised. No protected sample library is touched.

**Sound Core rules (unchanged)**
- No allocation, locks or logging on the audio thread.
- Denormals flushed (`DenormalGuard`).
- Sample-accurate MIDI.
- Every dial smoothed (`Smoothed`, 20 ms).
- Voices stolen quietest-first with a 20 ms fade.
- Output through `SpaceReverb`, `PeakLimiter` and `softLimit`.

**Instruments**
- **Tuning:** A = 440 Hz, accurate within **2 cents**. Fine tune **±50 cents** and Transpose **±12**, exact within **1 cent**.
- **Polyphony:**
  - Cabin Felt: **32 piano voices plus 1 horn**.
  - Morning Box: **24 box voices plus 24 wall voices**.
- **CPU (CI):**
  - Cabin Felt with a 12-note chord plus the horn: **< 6%** of real time.
  - Morning Box with a 12-note chord at the Build ceiling: **< 6%**.
  - Cabin or bedroom ambience at full: **< 2.5%**.
  - The existing combined budget: **< 8%**.

**Ambience**
- Ducking −4 dB while playing, about 2 s recovery. Glides 2.5 s.
- Panic never silences ambience.
- New sources go at the **end** of `ISDAmbienceSource`, so existing indices never move.

**Visuals**
- **Smooth rules:**
  - ≤ 1 MP
  - no per-pixel ray marching
  - noise ≤ 3 octaves
  - frosted-glass blur on High and Ultra only
  - no buffers made per frame
- Flares, glints, twinkles and glow swells go through `FlashLimiter` (sparkle halved when Gentle).
- No `pow` of a possibly negative base in any shader (`testShadersSquareWithoutPow` covers the new shader sources too).

**Credits**
- Text and a Spotify link only. No artwork, logos, photos, lyrics or music-video imagery.
- Card art is original, from the song's feeling, with tempo, key and track number as easter eggs.

**Usage**
- Build and review on **Opus at Extra (xhigh)**. **No Fable** anywhere in this run (Michael's instruction).

**Names**
- **Sets:**
  - `snowbound-cabin` / **Snowbound Cabin Window** / mood *Hushed, safe, warm* (`Mood.hushedWarm`)
  - `bedroom-dawn` / **Bedroom Window at Dawn** / mood *Soft, waking* (`Mood.softWaking`)
- **Instruments:** **Cabin Felt** (`cabinFelt`, subtype `isCF`, `ISD_ENGINE_CABIN_FELT = 4`) and **Morning Box** (`morningBox`, subtype `isMB`, `ISD_ENGINE_MORNING_BOX = 5`).

- **Hold:** `.pastelDawn` ("Pastel dawn", sun at **+1.5°**).

## Rulings recorded while planning

- **The Felt Glass fingerprint is a characterisation test.** It passes on today's code by design (it locks the sound before the refactor). The fingerprint numbers are measured with the local g++ mirror (`pad/build.sh`) from the unchanged engine, and the 0.1 dB / 1% tolerance absorbs compiler differences. *Cost if wrong:* a compiler difference larger than 1% shows as a RED on the first push, and is re-measured from CI's printed values before the refactor starts.
- **Speed limits under debug builds.** CI runs debug Swift, so the Core speed limits are asserted at their spec values only in optimised builds (`#if !DEBUG`), with ×10 headroom in debug:
  - frost field generation < 150 ms
  - cloth step < 0.3 ms

  The DSP target is optimised even in debug, so its budgets hold everywhere. *Cost if wrong:* a slow generator would only show on Michael's Mac (it runs off the main thread, with a fallback drawn meanwhile).
- **Test hooks:**
  - Parameters with unit 3 (hosts never show or save them):
    - `ISD_P_DEBUG_NO_MECHANICS = 202` and `ISD_P_DEBUG_HORN_ONLY = 203` (Cabin Felt)
    - `ISD_P_DEBUG_BOX_ONLY = 204` and `ISD_P_DEBUG_WALL_ONLY = 205` (Morning Box)
  - Debug counts: `ISD_DEBUG_HORN_NOTE = 1` (the horn's MIDI note, or −1), `ISD_DEBUG_BUILD_PERMILLE = 2` and `ISD_DEBUG_WALL_UNISON = 3`.
  - `isd_ambience_debug_start_kettle` starts a kettle cycle now. The 4–8 minute schedule is tested separately at density 1.5.
- **Order:**
  - Instruments first, so the preview builds reach Michael's ears early.
  - Then the sky and ambience (Tasks 6–9), so every world task wires against real sources and real weather.
  - Each card lands with its instrument's app task, as in the last run.
- **`SkyU` grows by one row** (index 12: halo, dawnPastel, gust, wetness). Every world binds `sky.uniforms`, so they all follow.
- **The frost field is 384 × 216 on every quality tier.** It's one texture read, so the tier only changes the patch budget and the blur.
- **Snow ticks are a mode of the existing rain-on-glass source** (`AmbienceContext.snowOnGlass`), not a new source.
- **The beach's wetness** rides in the spare lane `beachU[4].z`; the crest slots only use `.x` and `.y`. The road's goes in a new `roadU[5]`.
- **Pastel mornings** use the sky city's local calendar day: `floor((instant + zone offset) / 86400)`. In Quick day, that's the virtual instant, so each cycle is a new day.
- **Cabin "Hold the light"** is offered, defaulting to **Follow the sky**. The lake and lounge still don't offer it (spec §16).

### Task 2: Cabin Felt engine

**Files:**
- Create: `Sources/InStrumDSP/CabinFelt.{hpp,cpp}`, `Tests/InStrumAudioTests/DSP/CabinFeltTests.swift`
- Modify: `Sources/InStrumDSP/{include/InStrumDSP.h,Synth.cpp}`, `Tests/InStrumAudioTests/DSP/CPUBudgetTests.swift`

**Interfaces:**
- **C header:**
  - `ISD_ENGINE_CABIN_FELT = 4`.
  - Addresses: `ISD_P_HORN = 50`, `ISD_P_FIRE = 51`, `ISD_P_WOOD = 52` (plus `ISD_P_FELT = 10`, `ISD_P_SPACE = 2` and `ISD_P_WIDTH = 14`).
  - Hooks (unit 3): `ISD_P_DEBUG_NO_MECHANICS = 202` and `ISD_P_DEBUG_HORN_ONLY = 203`.
  - `ISDDebugCount` gains `ISD_DEBUG_HORN_NOTE = 1`, `ISD_DEBUG_BUILD_PERMILLE = 2` and `ISD_DEBUG_WALL_UNISON = 3`.
- **Parameter table, in this order:**
  - `fineTune`, `transpose`
  - `felt` .60, `horn` .30, `fire` .50, `wood` .50
  - `space` .35, `width` .50
  - the five `mood*`
  - `debugNoMechanics`, `debugHornOnly`
- **`class CabinFelt final : public Engine`:**
  - `kPolyphony = 32`, `kPool = 40`, `kSub = 32`.
  - Uses `StringBank<64>` and `SympBank` from Task 1.
  - `debugCount(ISD_DEBUG_HORN_NOTE)` returns the horn's current MIDI pitch, or −1.
- **Upright:** inharmonicity = `1.6 × inharmonicity(m)`; unison detune `d × 1.3`.
- **Hammer:**
  - contact `(4 − 3·v)` ms × `(1 + 1.5·felt)`
  - hammer cutoff × `(1 − 0.7·felt)`
  - level × `10^(−6·felt/20)` on the strike
- **Mechanics:**
  - key thump: band 60–120 Hz, 15 ms, about −20 dB re the note
  - damper lift: −40 dB, 8 ms
  - damper chuff on release: 10 ms, 300–900 Hz
  - pedal thump plus the sympathetic swell on pedal down
- **Body:** 4 cabinet peaks (about 95, 190, 360 and 720 Hz).
- **Room:** `wood` sets the early reflections (6 taps, 3–15 ms) and the Space reverb size 0.08–0.25.
- **Fire:** `tanh` saturation with drive `1 + 2·fire`, a low shelf +3·fire dB at 250 Hz and a high shelf −4·fire dB at 4 kHz.
- **Horn (one voice):**
  - Note choice, slur 75 ms, scoop −40 cents over 70 ms, release 130 ms: spec §4.1.
  - Range E3–C6 (MIDI 52–84), folded by octaves.
  - **Tone:** a sine-and-harmonics source through a dynamic waveshaper whose drive rises with velocity and over the attack (about 80 ms), then bell formants (peaks at 700 Hz and 1.3 kHz, Q about 2).
  - Breath noise band-passed around 1.5 kHz at −28 dB re the horn.
  - Vibrato after 0.5 s, 5 Hz, ±12 cents.
