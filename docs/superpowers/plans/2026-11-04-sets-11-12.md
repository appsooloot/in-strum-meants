# Sets 11 & 12 (Submarine Window, Great Barrier Reef) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ship Sets 11 and 12 ("Deep Water"), each with one of everything, plus two new instrument cores: an analogue voice and modal mallet bars. Real meteor showers reach every night sky, and sea smoke reaches every water world.

**Architecture:**
- The two engines are C++ behind the existing C API, each on a header-only core.
- The meteors are a seeded Core machine fed by the IMO calendar. They are drawn per pixel in `skyRadiance`, so reflections show them too.
- The two worlds follow the Core-model, App-renderer split, sharing a new Underwater Kit and a round Window Kit porthole.

**Tech stack:** Swift 6.1 (Swift 5 mode), SwiftUI, Metal (MSL in Swift strings), C++17 (`-O2`), SwiftPM, XCTest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-sets-11-12-design.md` (section numbers below refer to it).

**Builds on:** Sets 9 & 10 (`sets-9-10`). This branch is cut from it, and `sets-9-10` is merged in again whenever it moves. It brings pitch bend and aftertouch, the key in the ambience, optional credits and place cards, the `still` row, Sky extras, the paper-screen Window Kit material and rime.

## Review focus

1. **An arp left running.** Switching instrument, world or preset mid-arp, panic, or a pedal held through a switch must stop the arp and empty the echo within 50 ms. A new phrase then starts a fresh grid. Pinned in Task 1.
2. **Meteors at the extremes:**
   - the midnight sun (no darkness, so no meteors);
   - a circumpolar radiant (always up);
   - the date line and time zones: the calendar runs on UT solar longitude, and the local date is used only for display.

   Pinned in Task 5.
3. **Frozen and quick time.** A frozen peak night keeps showering, because rates are per real second. Quick mode walks the calendar day by day. Pinned in Task 5.
4. **Older saves.** These must still decode:
   - settings without `meteorRate`;
   - worlds without `meteors.mode`;
   - stored weather lists without `.seaSmoke`;
   - an older set store, which shows the 12-set gallery.

   Pinned in Tasks 5 and 13.
5. **Fireball bursts.** In "Always a shower" mode at rate 10, a run of bright meteors never pushes `meteorFlash` past `FlashLimiter`'s rules. Pinned in Task 5.
6. **Rolls and runs together.** With 4 rolling notes under a fast run, voice stealing stays clean (no clicks) and voices stay within 32. Pinned in Task 3.

## Rulings recorded while planning

Each ruling notes what it costs if it turns out wrong.
- **The arp lives in the engine** and runs sample-accurately. The visual follows through `SceneDirector.instrumentPulse`, not MIDI clock. Cost if wrong: none.
- **`meteorFlash` is limited in Core** by `LivingSky`'s own `FlashLimiter`, with the lightning's rules. Cost if wrong: none.
- **`SolarLongitude` is low-precision Meeus**, accurate to ±0.01°, about 15 minutes on a peak. Cost if wrong: none.
- **Meteors are evaluated per pixel in `skyRadiance`** (6 slots) rather than drawn as quads, so reflections get them for free. Cost if wrong: about 6 dot products per sky pixel, nearly all rejected at once.
- **Each renderer computes its own water band** from its geometry every frame. The WeatherLayer only draws steam inside it. Cost if wrong: none.
- **`MeteorMode.spec` is added to every night-sky world's parameters** in Task 5: lake, beach, road, cabin, bedroom, fjord, bay, tea house and garden. Cost if wrong: one line each.
- **RED by construction**, as in the earlier runs: tests that reference new symbols cannot build before the implementation exists.
