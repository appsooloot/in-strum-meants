# Sound brief: Cabin Felt (after "Stay")

## Facts
- **Song:** "Stay", Mac Miller. *The Divine Feminine* (2016), **track 3** (5:26). [Wikipedia: The Divine Feminine][tdf]
- **Producer:** **ID Labs**. **Trumpet: Keyon Harrold** (the official video's credits say the same). Additional vocals: Paige Montgomery. [tdf], [yt]
- **Key:** **F♯ major**. [Tunebat][tb], [SongBPM][sb], [Musicstax][ms]
- **Tempo:** **134 BPM** in 4/4. It's felt in half time, about **67**: "a slow, relaxed pocket that feels intimate and almost hypnotic." [sb], [gsb]
- **The sound:**
  - A smooth, tranquil production with gentle beats and **mellow trumpet accents**. [miw]
  - In "Stay", "trumpets and vocals fuse together. The trumpets bounce around and resonate with the flowing melody." [bch]
  - One review hears it as straight off *Surf*: horn licks riding over warm, laid-back keys. [misc]
  - The keys are soft and warm. The trumpet carries the melody's answers, scooping and slurring like a jazz player.
- **Spotify search:** `Stay Mac Miller`

## What we're matching
- **Register:**
  - The keys sit around F♯2–F♯5.
  - The horn's phrases sit around C♯4–F♯5 (a flugelhorn's sweet range is about E3–C6).
- **Piano tone colour:**
  - A hushed, close felt-piano strike.
  - Spectral centroid at mf (velocity 80) on **F♯4 (370 Hz)**, felt 0.6: **0.6–1.2 kHz over the first 80 ms**.
  - A hard strike (velocity 120) at least 1.3× brighter than a soft one (40).
- **Piano attack:** under **5 ms** (felt contact 1–4 ms × the felt).
- **Piano decay:**
  - With the pedal down, a middle note (F♯4) rings **4–10 s** to −40 dB. An upright is shorter than a grand, and the felt shortens it further.
  - With no pedal, a release damps in about 150 ms with a soft felt "chuff".
- **Mechanics:**
  - a key thump at 60–120 Hz, about 15 ms, about −20 dB re the note
  - the damper chuff at 300–900 Hz on release
  - all audible up close, never louder than the note
- **Horn:**
  - **Tone:** a dark, soft flugelhorn. Steady-state centroid on F♯4 at mf: **0.9–1.6 kHz**, brighter when played harder (≥ 1.25× from velocity 40 to 120).
  - **Scoop:** fresh notes start about **40 cents flat** and reach pitch in about **70 ms**.
  - **Slur:** legato moves glide in **60–90 ms**, with no gap.
  - **Vibrato:** delayed about 0.5 s, then about **5 Hz, ±12 cents**.
  - **Breath:** band-passed around 1.5 kHz, about −28 dB.
- **Space:** a small log room (early reflections at 3–15 ms, RT60 about 0.6–1.2 s) and the Space reverb behind it.

## What we'll do better
- **The horn follows your melody by itself.** Play chords with a top line and the horn doubles only the top line, slurring when you play legato. There's no separate horn track to perform.
- **Every part is a dial:** Felt (the moderator), Horn, Fire (warmth), Wood (the room).
- **Velocity shapes everything:** the hammer's hardness and the horn's brightness.
