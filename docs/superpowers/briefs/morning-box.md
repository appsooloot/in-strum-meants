# Sound brief: Morning Box (after "Dunno")

## Facts
- **Song:** "Dunno", Mac Miller. *Swimming* (2018), **track 10** (3:57). [Wikipedia: Swimming][sw]
- **Producers:** **Parson Brown**, with additional production by **Mac Miller and Jon Brion**. Programming: Parson Brown and Jon Brion (Apple Music credits). [sw], [am]
- **Key:** **C♯ major**. [Tunebat][tb], [AcousticBrainz][ab]
- **Tempo:** **82 BPM**. [tb], [ab] (Tunebat also lists a live take at 85.)
- **The sound:**
  - "Simple sounding with some beautiful synths that slowly build up to a wall of solace and tranquility." [aoty]
  - A gentle, restrained production: smooth synths, minimal beats, a hazy and relaxed texture. [miw]
  - A **ticking** sound runs through it. [imn]
- **Spotify search:** `Dunno Mac Miller`

## What we're matching
- **Register:**
  - The synths sit around C♯3–C♯5.
  - The box (celesta or music box) sparkles above them, around C♯4–C♯7.
- **Celesta bars:**
  - modes at about 1, 2.76, 5.40 and 8.93 × f₀, with the upper modes dying fast
  - on **C♯5 (554 Hz)**, a ring of **2–4 s** to −40 dB (pedal down), shorter higher up
  - a felt **tick** at 2–4 kHz, gone within about **5 ms** (at least 20 dB down by 15 ms)
  - dampers stop a released note within about 80 ms
- **Music box comb** (Box 1):
  - modes at about 1, 6.27 and 17.55 × f₀
  - a ring of **4–8 s**
  - a small wooden box resonance at 300–900 Hz
- **Synth wall:**
  - attack **0.4–1.2 s**, release about 1.5 s
  - warm: a centroid under about 1 kHz at the start of a phrase
- **Build** (the wall growing as you play):
  - **rise τ 20–40 s** (the Build dial)
  - **fall τ about 12 s** in silence
  - at full build: centroid at least 1.4× and stereo width at least 1.2× the start
- **Haze:**
  - chorus at **0.3–0.6 Hz**
  - a soft tape blur (a slight high roll-off, ±3 cents wow)
- **Space:** soft and medium-sized, never washy.

## What we'll do better
- **The wall builds from your playing.** Keep playing and the synths thicken: more voices, an octave, a soft shimmer. They thin out gently when you stop. You don't need an arrangement for it.
- **One dial morphs celesta into music box.**
- **Every part is a dial:** Bars (the strike), Box, Bloom (the wall), Build, Haze.

## Play-along notes
- **Key:** C♯ major (seven sharps). C♯, F♯ and G♯ chords, with A♯m colour.
- **Feel:** a slow walk at 82.
- **Tuning:** concert pitch.
