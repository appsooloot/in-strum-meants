# Sound brief: Polar Choir (after "Wings")

## Facts
- **Song:** "Wings", Mac Miller. *Swimming* (2018), **track 6** (4:10). [Wikipedia: Swimming][sw]
- **Producer:** **Alexander Spit**. [sw]
- **Key:** **A♭ major**. [Tunebat][tb]
- **Tempo:** **119 BPM**, a half-time feel of about 60. [tb], [GetSongBPM][gs] (SongData lists B♭ minor at 90 for the same track; the two sites that agree win.)
- **The sound:** airy, slowly swelling synth pads under a floating vocal, with wide space and a feeling of lift. The pads breathe rather than attack; the harmony hangs on long chords.
- **Spotify search:** `Wings Mac Miller`

## What we're matching
- **Register:** the pads sit around A♭2–A♭5; the glass layer rings above, A♭4–A♭6.
- **Voices (vowel pad):**
  - three formants per voice, morphing u → o → a → e → i with the Vowel dial; at Vowel 0.5 (/a/) on A3 (220 Hz) the F1 peak sits near **700 Hz** and F2 near **1220 Hz**, within 6%; at Vowel 0 (/u/) F1 near 325 Hz; at Vowel 1 (/i/) F2 above 2 kHz
  - a slow per-voice vowel drift (±0.06 of the dial, 0.05–0.12 Hz) so a chord never sits still
  - formants key-track 20%
- **Breath:** noise through the same formants; Breath 1 against 0 raises the 2–6 kHz floor by **≥ 6 dB** on a held chord
- **Swell:** attack 0.3–3.0 s by the dial; while held the level keeps rising for about 4 s (**≥ 3 dB** from 0.5–1.0 s to 4.0–4.5 s at Swell 1), then eases back over 2 s after release; release 1.5–5 s
- **Glass:** sines at 1, 2, 3 and **4.2 × f₀** (the 4.2 partial within 2%, ≥ 12 dB over its absence) with a 0.6–1.5 s onset and slow unison beating
- **Shimmer:** the Space reverb's octave-up tail
- **Tuning:** within 2 cents on the saws' fundamental.
- **Panic:** all-notes-off ends a swell within 50 ms; nothing sounds by itself afterwards.
- **CPU:** a 12-note chord under 6% of real time (CI).

## What we'll do better
- **Vowels you can play:** one dial moves the whole choir from a dark /u/ to a bright /i/, with breath that reads as air in a room full of voices.
- **A swell that answers holding:** the longer you hold, the more the choir leans in, like a bow pressing; let go and it settles.
- **Bowed glass on top** for the aurora's cold shine, as its own dial.
- **Every part is a dial:** Vowel, Breath, Glass, Swell, Shimmer, Space, Width.
